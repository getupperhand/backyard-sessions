07 · Capability two

Not influencers. Songwriters. Each artist takes a Solo product home and turns it into original sound and footage, in their own backyard, in their own voice.

Phase 0 · the pilot

One backyard, North Shore

A real home on Chicago's North Shore. One night, one to three artists, full capture. Small enough to get right.

Phase 1 · season one

Four nights, same neighborhood

Same host, a new lineup each night. The format finds its rhythm and paid gets a month of fresh creative from every night.

Phase 2 · artist backyards

The artist hosts at home

A subset moves to the artist's own yard. Their place, their people, their town. The series and the creator program in one shoot.

Phase 3 · cities

City by city

Nashville, Austin, Denver, and home turf in DFW. Local artists, local retail partners, the same fire ritual.
